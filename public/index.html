How Instant Bingo works

Shared live round — everyone sees the same countdown and the same 20 numbers (like classic rooms).

1. Select up to 4 cards and a stake during the timer. Same card numbers are allowed for different players.

2. Play locks your cards and charges stake × number of cards from your balance (same wallet as Classic Bingo).

3. Draw — when the timer hits 0, 20 numbers are called (1 second each). Matching cells highlight on your cards.

Winning (best pattern per card)
• 1 row or 1 column → ×1 (stake back)
• 2 rows/columns → ×2
• 1 diagonal → ×1.5
• Four corners → ×2.5
• Four corners + any line → ×4
Wins credit to your balance automatically. Strikes appear live during the draw.

Watch — you can watch the live draw without paying. After the draw, everyone returns to selection for the next round.
